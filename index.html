Selected projects

May – July 2026 · NISER summer internship

Evolution of galactic magnetic fields

How does a galaxy sustain a magnetic field across its disc? I modelled the α–Ω dynamo in Python, using finite differences in space and Runge–Kutta integration in time to follow the field as the disc rotated.

NumPy · SciPy · Matplotlib · Numba
