Research projects

Evolution of Galactic Magnetic Fields: A Mean-Field Dynamo Model
NISER Bhubaneswar · Summer research internship · May–July 2026

Modelled the α–Ω dynamo, differential rotation, turbulent magnetic diffusivity and magnetic-field amplification in galactic discs. Implemented finite-difference solutions with Runge–Kutta time integration in Python and analysed the spatial and temporal evolution of the field.

Electromagnetic Radiation from Astronomical Sources
Investigatory project · December 2024–February 2025

Studied emission across wavelength regimes and how spectra reveal the physical properties of galaxies and the interstellar medium.
